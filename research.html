What I work on

I study how biological systems predict future events, make decisions, and learn. My work combines computational neuroscience, machine learning, neural networks, and statistical modeling to understand how evolving neural states generate complex behavior.

PhD research

Dynamic Cortical Interactions Underlying Cognitive Motor Behaviors

I analyzed large-scale neural and behavioral recordings to characterize neural interactions between frontal, motor, and parietal areas during anticipation, decision making and learning.

Deep learning projects

Hand Trajectory Prediction

Predicting where a movement is heading before it ends. Using only the beginning of a hand movement, the project addresses three related tasks:

  • Endpoint prediction: estimate the final hand position
  • Target classification: identify the intended target among alternatives
  • Trajectory forecasting: generate the remaining movement
